# Battle Phase Glossary Addendum v1

Owner-approved companion to Wandbound_Rules_Bible_Battle_Phase_Addendum_v1.md.

## Battle Phase

Temporary phase created by a legal player Declared Attack and lasting through
complete resolution or cancellation of the root Attack and resulting Counterattack.
It is independent nested state, not a replacement for Action/Response priority.

Counter does not create another Battle. Battle is not once per turn.
Normal Action actions are unavailable unless specifically permitted at the current
Battle timing. Accepted declarations create Battle even if later prevented.
Nonterminal completion returns to Action; terminal completion ends with the match.
NPC/automatic roots remain outside the initial scope.

## During a Battle

While authoritative BattlePhase is active, including its PreHit, nested reactions,
damage processing, AfterDamage, PostHit, and resulting Counterattack.
This condition alone grants neither timing nor priority.

"At the end of a Battle" is not canonized as an automatic trigger checkpoint here.
Battle-end triggers remain deferred.
