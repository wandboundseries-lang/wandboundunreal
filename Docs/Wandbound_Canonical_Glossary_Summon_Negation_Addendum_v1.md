# Wandbound Canonical Glossary Addendum: Summon Negation

## Declare Summon

A player intentionally chooses an exact Character and a legal destination for a normal summon. The declaration creates the Pre-Summon response timing; it does not itself place the unit.

## Pending Summon

A declared summon awaiting completion of its Pre-Summon response window. The exact selected card remains in its authoritative source zone until the summon succeeds. A pending summon is not a successful summon.

## Pre-Summon

The response timing after a legal Declared Summon and before successful placement. The opponent receives first response priority under the established reaction rules.

## Negate a Summon

Cancel a pending summon before the unit is successfully summoned. A negated summon is not a successful summon and does not by itself destroy, sacrifice, damage, or move the attempted card to another zone.

Negate a Summon does not mean negate an effect. The two mechanics have separate typed targets.

## When This Unit Is Summoned

A condition satisfied only after the unit has been successfully summoned. It is not satisfied by declaring a summon, creating a pending summon, opening Pre-Summon, or negating a summon.
